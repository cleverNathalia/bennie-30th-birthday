The arrival moment — an arcade attract screen. Full-bleed still, blinking prompt, any input enters the rack.

```jsx
<AttractScreen image="assets/bennie-portrait.png" subtitle="Cabinet No. 30"
  title={<>PLAYER ONE<br/>TURNS THIRTY</>} onStart={enter} />
```

- The still runs at 50% opacity under a heavy radial darkening — it sets mood, it is not the subject.
- A soft cyan band sweeps down every 5.5s (the CRT refresh). Slow and barely visible; don't brighten it.
- Show it once per session and remember the dismissal — it must never become a wall between him and the day's gift.
