Opening a cartridge does NOT open a modal — the whole page becomes that day. `DayView` is that page.

```jsx
<DayView day={3} title="Thirty true things" onClose={back}>
  <DayText>One for every year.</DayText>
  <DayList items={things} />
  <DayScreen caption="Filmed 04 Sep"><iframe src={url} …/></DayScreen>
</DayView>
```

- Enters with a CRT power-up: a radial bloom scales open from a horizontal line over 620ms.
- Three exits, all equal: Escape, the pink ✕ top-right, and "◀ Back to the rack" at the bottom.
- Body copy here carries NO glow and a 62ch measure — the day's content is the one place the design gets out of the way.
