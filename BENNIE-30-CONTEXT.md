## The situation

Danielle (Cape Town) is missing her husband **Bennie's 30th birthday on Thursday 10 September 2026**. He is in Dublin; her visa didn't come through in time. She wants to give him **one thing every day** from 3 to 10 September — a song one day, a poem she wrote the next, and so on.

**Eight days total:** Thu 3 Sept → Thu 10 Sept inclusive.

## Bennie

- Married Danielle **11 April 2026**. They met in high school and have known each other **13 years**.
- Moved to Dublin around April/May 2026 — apart roughly **five months**, since not long after the wedding.
- Master's in electrical engineering. Works in an office in Dublin.
- Loves: anime, factory/PC games (**Satisfactory**), DnD, board games. Watches **Veritasium** and anything he can learn from.
- Sweet tooth. Loves pizza. Minimalist — not a stuff person.
- Extremely intelligent and funny, lights up a room, everyone loves him, would give the shirt off his back.
- Free Saturdays: out with friends finding new hangouts for board games or new food — or home gaming, reading, doing laundry.
- Has a housemate (very introverted), colleagues and a few friends in Dublin.
- **What he has actually said he misses:** cuddles, Danielle bringing him coffee in the morning, laughing with her, sleeping together, having his best friend with him.

## Shared

- They both love **EPIC: The Musical** (Jorge Rivera-Herrans), but have **no specific song** that is "theirs" — the plan deliberately creates one.
- Danielle plays board games with him. She used to play DnD, no longer does.

## Danielle — what she can do

- Sings, writes, comfortable on camera.
- Photos are in Google Photos (can use its AI/search to find things). Old voice notes exist but are impractical to dig out.
- Budget: **R1 500** total (≈ €80 at R1 = €0.0535, 3 Sept).
- Bennie **suspects nothing**.

## Decisions already made

1. **Just the two of them.** No recruiting friends, family or colleagues. A crowd-sourced video from 30 people was proposed and Danielle deliberately rejected it — she wants it kept private and special. Do not re-suggest it.
2. **Everything comes from her.** Claude plans, structures and prompts; Claude does **not** write the poem, the letter or the thirty things. Prompts and opening lines only.
3. **She tells him on day one** that something is coming every day.
4. **Tone: a mix** — playful early, building to tender.
5. **Physical items ordered online direct to his door** (no one on the ground helping).
6. **Delivery vessel:** a private web page for Bennie where **one new door unlocks each day** — her idea, and she loved it. WhatsApp is the daily nudge ("door 4 is open"). Still to be built.

### Key risk

**Test the card this week.** A declined international payment at 07:15 on his birthday is the one unrecoverable failure. Put a small order through an Irish app on Friday to confirm it clears.

## Design principles behind the plan

- **The coffee is a two-part gesture** — the filmed version on Friday sets up the real delivery on Thursday morning. Never explain Friday.
- **Presence is the only one of the five things he misses that can be delivered from 9 000 km away** — hence Wednesday's do-nothing call.
- **Every day has a three-minute fallback** so a collapsed evening never breaks the streak.
- **Same time every day.** The ritual is worth more than any single item in it.
- **Never apologise for a small day. Never explain a gesture.**
